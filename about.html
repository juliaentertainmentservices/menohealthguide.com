ABOUT

Clear menopause information.

MenoHealthGuide is being built as an educational resource for people looking for understandable information about menopause and the transition around it.

Our approach

Articles are designed to explain topics in plain language, distinguish general information from medical advice, and encourage readers to discuss personal symptoms and treatment decisions with qualified healthcare professionals.

Editorial note

Health information can change as evidence develops. Automated publishing will be designed with topic controls, source fields and review safeguards rather than blindly publishing unsupported claims.

Medical disclaimer

This website does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or replace a consultation with a healthcare professional. If you have urgent or concerning symptoms, seek appropriate medical care.
