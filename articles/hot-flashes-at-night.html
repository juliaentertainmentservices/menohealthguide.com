Hot Flashes at Night: What to Know

Published August 22, 2026 • Educational guide

Nighttime hot flashes and sweating can interrupt sleep. Keeping a simple symptom record may help you identify patterns and have a more useful healthcare conversation.

Things that may be useful to track

  • Time of night and frequency
  • Bedroom temperature and bedding
  • Foods, drinks or activities before bed
  • Sleep quality the following day

Comfort strategies

Some people find a cooler bedroom, breathable sleepwear, layered bedding and a consistent sleep routine helpful. If symptoms are frequent or significantly affecting daily life, discuss them with a healthcare professional.

Medical note: General educational information only. Individual symptoms can have multiple causes and should be evaluated appropriately.
